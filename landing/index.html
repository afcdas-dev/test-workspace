Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. The template works on Notion's free plan. If your whole team collaborates inside it daily, Notion's own paid plans add more guests and version history, but the system itself requires nothing extra.

I'm not a Notion expert. Can I set this up?

Yes — that's the point of buying a system instead of building one. Everything is pre-built and pre-linked; the included guide walks you through adding your clients and team in about 10 minutes. If you can fill in a form, you can run this.

Will this work for my agency's size?

The system is designed for freelancers scaling up, and for agencies of roughly 2–20 people. The structure is the same whether you manage 3 clients or 30 — the databases simply grow with you.

How is this different from free Notion templates?

Free templates are usually isolated pages — a CRM here, a task list there. This is one connected system: clients link to projects, projects to tasks, tasks to your dashboard. The value is in the connections, which take weeks to build correctly from scratch.
